Why Artificial Intelligence?

In today's world, enormous amounts of data are generated every second. Humans cannot efficiently process and analyze such large volumes of information.

AI helps solve this problem by:

Processing data quickly
Finding hidden patterns
Making predictions
Automating repetitive tasks
Improving decision-making

Without AI, many modern applications such as recommendation systems, voice assistants, and self-driving vehicles would not be practical.

How AI Works

AI systems learn from data and use algorithms to identify patterns.

A simplified AI workflow:

Data → Learning Algorithm → Model → Predictions/Decisions

For example:

Thousands of cat images are provided.
The system learns common patterns.
When shown a new image, it predicts whether it contains a cat.
Characteristics of AI
Learning

AI systems improve performance by learning from data and experience.

Reasoning

AI can analyze information and make logical decisions.

Problem Solving

AI can evaluate multiple possibilities and determine the best solution.

Perception

AI can understand images, videos, sounds, and other sensory information.

Language Understanding

AI can understand and generate human language.

Types of Artificial Intelligence
Narrow AI

Narrow AI is designed to perform a specific task.

Examples:

ChatGPT
Siri
Google Translate
Netflix Recommendations

Most AI systems today belong to this category.

General AI

General AI refers to a machine capable of performing any intellectual task that a human can perform.

Such systems do not currently exist.

Super AI

Super AI is a hypothetical form of intelligence that exceeds human intelligence in every aspect.

Currently theoretical.
